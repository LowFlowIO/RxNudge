# RxNudge – User Guide

## Please read first
**RxNudge is a reminder aid. It is not a medical device.**
- Always follow your doctor's and pharmacist's instructions and the medicine leaflet.
- Do not rely on RxNudge alone for medicines where a missed or extra dose is dangerous. Keep a second way of
  checking (a pill box, a paper list, a person who checks on you).
- A reminder can fail: the battery can run flat, the device can break, the clock can be wrong, or the sound can be
  too quiet to hear. If you are not sure whether you took a dose, **do not take another one**. Ask your pharmacist or
  doctor.
- RxNudge never tells you to take a missed dose later or to take two doses at once.
- In an emergency call your local emergency number (112 in Europe).

## What RxNudge does
RxNudge sits on a table or the fridge and shows whose medicine is due. When a dose is due it beeps and the screen
flashes. You confirm that you took it, and RxNudge keeps a record. Several people in one household can use it,
each with their own medicines and reminders.

The screen is e-paper, like an e-book reader: it keeps showing the last picture even when the device sleeps, and
it changes with a short flicker.

## The buttons
- **Touch screen:** tap buttons on the screen.
- **UP / DOWN** (side buttons): move between buttons.
- **OK / AI** button: press the selected button. Also wakes the device.
- **Hold OK for 2 seconds** on the home screen: the device goes to sleep. It still wakes up for reminders.

## First start
A short setup guides you through:
1. **Welcome:** read the note and tap **I understand**.
2. **Language:** English.
3. **Time zone:** Europe/Berlin is preselected.
4. **Wi-Fi (optional):** lets the device keep its clock exact. You can skip this.
5. **Clock:** if Wi-Fi is set up, RxNudge fetches the time from the internet by itself (the black
   **Get time from internet** button does it again). Otherwise tap **Set date and time** (see "Entering dates and
   times" below). Reminders start only once the clock is set.
6. **First person:** type a name.
7. **PIN (optional):** a PIN stops others from changing medicines and settings.
8. **Import (optional):** restore an earlier backup from a microSD card, or start fresh.
9. **Ready.** Add medicines next: **Menu > Medications**.

## Entering dates and times
Dates and times are shown as separate boxes: **day, month, year** and **hour : minute**. The line underneath shows
the result in words, for example "Mon 19 Oct 2026, 08:30" — check it before you save.
- **Arrows:** tap the big arrow above a box to count up, below it to count down.
- **Typing:** tap a box and a number pad appears. Type two digits (four for the year); RxNudge jumps to the next
  box by itself. **Del** removes a digit, **OK** finishes the box.
- **Side buttons:** UP / DOWN change the black (selected) box, OK moves to the next box; after the last box, OK saves.
- Days that do not exist are corrected: 31 April becomes 30 April, 29 February only in leap years.
- The same boxes are used for medicine start and end dates, reminder times and "When was it taken?".

## Adding a medicine
1. On the home screen tap **Menu**, then **Medications**, then add one.
2. Enter the name, the dose (for example "1 tablet") and who it is for.
3. **Times** are listed right below the dose. A new medicine starts with one time, **Time 1  08:00**.
   - Tap **Time 1** to change it: tap **At** and type the hours and minutes (for example 1 4 3 5 for 14:35).
   - Tap **+ Add time** to add another time; the hours and minutes boxes open straight away.
   - On a time's page you can instead choose **Anchor**: a routine such as Breakfast or Bed, with an offset in
     minutes. The time then follows the person's routine times.
   - **Delete this time** (tap twice to confirm) removes it. The last time cannot be deleted.
4. **Schedule** sets the days (every day, certain weekdays, every few days, a cycle, or "as needed" with a daily
   maximum) and, for tapering, several phases. With several phases each phase has its own times list.
5. Optional: number of tablets left, so RxNudge can warn you before you run out, and instructions such as "with
   food". Tick **Important** for medicines that must also sound during quiet hours.
6. Save.

Touch: a tap counts when you lift your finger, once. After the screen changes, lift your finger before tapping
again; a tap made while the screen is still changing is ignored.

Ask your pharmacist if you are unsure how to enter a schedule. Check the **Today** list afterwards to see that the
times are right.

## When a reminder sounds
The screen shows the person, the medicine and the dose.
- **TAKEN:** you took it. Tap it, then tap again to confirm ("TAP AGAIN"), or hold OK for 1 second. This prevents
  accidental taps.
- **SNOOZE:** remind me again in 5, 10 or 15 minutes (up to 3 times).
- **SKIP:** you are not taking this dose. It is recorded as skipped.
- **UNDO:** if you tapped the wrong thing, undo it shortly afterwards.
- If nobody responds, RxNudge repeats the reminder a few times. After a while the dose is recorded as **Missed**.
- If you took it but forgot to tap, open **Today**, choose the dose, tap **EARLIER** and enter the hour and minute it
  was really taken (the line below shows the day and how long ago). RxNudge refuses this if it is too close to the
  next dose, to avoid a double dose.

If several people have doses due at once, RxNudge reminds them one after another.

## The screens
- **Home:** each person's next dose and how many are done today. Messages at the top tell you about problems.
- **Today:** all doses of the day with their state (Due, Taken, Skipped, Missed). Here are also the
  **As needed** medicines: tap **LOG DOSE** when you take one. It shows when the next dose is allowed.
- **History:** day, week and month view, per medicine, with how often doses were on time.
- **Aa:** larger text.

## Messages you may see
| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| Clock not set - reminders paused | Menu > Settings > Set clock |
| Clock may be wrong - please check | Check the time; set it again or use Wi-Fi |
| Battery low - please charge | Connect the USB-C cable |
| Battery empty. Reminders will stop until it is charged. | Charge now, then press the AI button. No reminders happen until then |
| No SD card - backups paused | Insert a microSD card for the nightly backups, or turn Nightly backup off (Menu > Settings > Backup & restore) |
| Backup failed - check SD card | The card is full or faulty. Free space or use another card; RxNudge tries again within the hour |
| SD card error | Take the card out and put it back, or try another card |
| Storage error - data may be lost | Make a backup, write down your medicines and contact the person who set up the device |
| Buzzer fault - visual alerts only | The device cannot beep. Look at it regularly or get it checked |
| Quiet hours - silent | Normal reminders do not beep now; Important ones still do |
| N missed while asleep - see History | Reminders came while the battery was empty or the device was off. Do not take extra doses; ask your pharmacist if unsure |

## Charging and battery
Charge with the USB-C cable when the battery is low; a full charge lasts several days (the exact time depends on how
often reminders sound and has not been measured yet). Keep the device charged: with an empty battery there are no
reminders.

## Wi-Fi (optional)
Wi-Fi is off unless you turn it on. It is used only to keep the clock exact, to send an optional "missed dose"
message to a caregiver, and for the web page below. RxNudge works fully without Wi-Fi.
- **Set up:** Menu > Settings > Wi-Fi > Phone hotspot. Connect your phone to the network shown on the screen with the
  password shown, then pick your home network on the page that opens.
- **Test:** "Test connection" shows "Connecting... N s" while it runs; you can keep using the device.

## Web page (optional)
Menu > Settings > Web portal shows an address and a one-time code. Open the address on a phone or computer in the
same Wi-Fi and enter the code to edit medicines, download records or install updates. It turns itself off after 10
minutes.

## Backups
Keep a microSD card in the device and RxNudge backs up your medicines and records **every night** by itself
(shortly after 3 o'clock). If the device was asleep or off then, it does it the next time it wakes up.
- It only makes a new backup when something changed, and at least once a week.
- The last 14 nightly backups are kept; older nightly ones are deleted. Backups you make yourself are never deleted.
- Wi-Fi passwords are never put in a backup.
- **See when it last worked:** Menu > Settings > Backup & restore shows "Last automatic" and the status.
  You can turn **Nightly backup** off there (it is on unless you turn it off).
- **Make one now:** same menu, **Back up now** (do this after changing medicines if you want to be safe today).
- **Restore:** same menu, or the setup guide. The list shows nightly ("auto-…") and your own ("backup-…") backups,
  newest first.
- No backup is made while the battery is almost empty; it is done after charging.

## Privacy
Records stay on the device and the SD card. Exports contain medicine names only if you choose so. With a PIN set you
can hide names on the home screen.

## Getting help
Write down what the screen shows and contact the person who set up the device.
